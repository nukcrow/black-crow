Privacy Policy — NukCrow Connect Last Updated: September 19, 2026 NukCrow Connect (“the Bot”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Bot and how that information is used. 1. Information We Collect When you interact with the Bot, we may receive and store information provided by Telegram, including: * Telegram User ID * Username * First name * Date and time of interactions * Bot usage statistics * Messages sent to the Bot, when necessary to provide support * Files, photos, videos, voice messages, or other content voluntarily sent to the support system We do not request passwords, payment card information, or Telegram login credentials. 2. How We Use Information Information may be used to: * Provide and operate the Bot’s features * Deliver configuration files and services requested by users * Check whether a user is authorized to use certain features * Maintain basic usage statistics * Respond to support requests * Prevent abuse or unauthorized use * Manage blocked or restricted users 3. Data Sharing We do not intentionally sell or rent personal information to third parties. The Bot operates through Telegram and may use infrastructure or services required to operate its features, such as GitHub and Cloudflare. Information may be processed by these services as necessary for the Bot to function. 4. User Messages and Support If you voluntarily contact the Bot’s support system, your message and any attachments you send may be forwarded to the Bot administrator so that your request can be handled. Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, financial information, or other sensitive information through the Bot. 5. Data Storage and Retention We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, provide support, maintain security, and keep basic usage records. Some technical data may be stored automatically by the services used to operate the Bot. 6. Data Deletion If you want information associated with your Telegram account to be deleted, you may contact the Bot administrator through the available support channel. Requests will be reviewed and handled where technically and legally possible. 7. Children’s Privacy The Bot is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to operating the Bot. 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date. 9. Contact For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the Bot administrator through the official support method provided by NukCrow Connect
